Getting startedWhat do I need to set up my company?+

Have your company name, industry, admin email and a strong password ready. Register from the FlavorFlow website. Save the company code, server address and trial end date from your registration confirmation. Keep your password private.

Link to this article
Getting startedWhere should my team begin?+

Choose the industry relevant to your factory. Before entering day-to-day transactions, review your units, packing categories and company settings. Confirm opening stock and responsibilities with your company admin or accounts team.

Link to this article
Getting startedHow does the trial work?+

Registration starts your company’s trial. Your confirmation shows the end date, and current plan details are available on the main website. Store publication is still pending; confirm app access before registering if you cannot use the app yet. Registration does not authorise automatic debits.

Link to this article
Account & accessWhere can I find my company code?+

Use the company code from your registration confirmation. Company codes identify the company workspace; they are not your password. If you do not have the confirmation, ask your company admin rather than trying another organisation’s code.

Link to this article
Account & accessWhat should I check if sign-in fails?+

Check your company code or server address, internet connection and admin email. Enter your password carefully and use the correct company workspace. If you need help, note the error message and app version. Never send your password, OTP or recovery codes to support.

Link to this article
Account & accessWhy can’t a team member see a section?+

Sections and actions may depend on the user’s assigned role and permissions. Ask your company admin to review that user’s access. Do not share an admin account to bypass permissions.

Link to this article
Production & inventoryWhat should I check when stock looks incorrect?+

Compare the item and unit against receipts, production consumption, completed goods and dispatch entries. Review the stock ledger and the relevant dates before changing records. Record the affected item and transaction references for your company admin.

Link to this article
Production & inventoryWhat information helps with a batch issue?+

Note the batch reference, item, production date, expected quantity and recorded quantity. Check the related raw-material consumption and completed-goods entries. Share only a redacted screenshot if a support case is needed.

Link to this article
Production & inventoryHow should opening stock be reviewed?+

Agree the opening date and physical stock quantities with your accounts or operations team. Check item names, units and any batch information before recording them. Avoid adding the same stock both as an opening balance and as a new receipt.

Link to this article
Dispatch & documentsWhat should I check before dispatch?+

Confirm the destination, selected batches, quantities and vehicle/loading information. Review the dispatch record before producing the challan. Use your organisation’s approval process where applicable.

Link to this article
Dispatch & documentsA challan or PDF won’t open. What can I check?+

Try opening the file in a PDF viewer and confirm that the download completed. Check the dispatch reference and file permissions on your device. If the problem continues, note your app version, device type and the exact error; redact customer details from screenshots.

Link to this article
Dispatch & documentsWhich reference should I include for a dispatch issue?+

Include the dispatch or challan reference, date and a short description of the expected result. State whether the issue affects one shipment or several. Do not attach full customer lists or confidential transport documents unless requested through a verified support channel.

Link to this article
Billing & subscriptionsWhat should I review on a GST invoice?+

Check company and party details, GSTIN, state, item values and applicable tax treatment before finalising an invoice. Ask your accounts team or tax adviser to confirm compliance questions. Technical support is not a substitute for tax advice.

Link to this article
Billing & subscriptionsHow can my company pay for a subscription?+

Request a company invoice and verified beneficiary details. Bank/offline options include NEFT, IMPS, RTGS (minimum ₹2 lakh) and cheque; activation follows verification or cheque clearance. Online UPI/net banking and optional mandates are not enabled yet. Credit/debit cards are not offered. Check the main website for current payment instructions.

Link to this article
Billing & subscriptionsWhat details are needed to verify a payment?+

Keep your company code, invoice number, amount, date and UTR/payment reference or cheque details. Verify beneficiary details before paying. Do not share banking passwords, PINs, OTPs or full account statements. Online checkout and automatic bank-payment matching remain pending.

Link to this article
Reports & downloadsWhere can I install the Android app?+

The public Android installation route is Google Play. Publication is pending, so there is no live store button or installation QR yet. Once approved, the button and QR on the main website will open the same official listing. Do not install APKs from unofficial links.

Link to this article
Reports & downloadsIs the Windows download available?+

The Windows download is coming soon. The main website retains a download option, but an official release link has not been published. This support centre does not offer a Windows installer or claim that the desktop edition is released.

Link to this article
Reports & downloadsWhat should I check if an export is missing?+

Review the selected report, date range and any item or batch filters. Supported reports can be exported as PDF or Excel; available options depend on the report. Check your device’s downloaded files and include the report name and filters if you need help.
