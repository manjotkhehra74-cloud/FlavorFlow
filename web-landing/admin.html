How to activate a cheque payment

  1. Customer picks a plan → Set plan (creates a due invoice FF-INV-…; the period starts the day after the trial / current paid period ends).
  2. Cheque clears / NEFT received → Mark paid with cheque no + bank → access moves to the invoice period end automatically.
  3. Cheque late but promised → Extend a few days. Cheque bounced → Suspend (blocks the app instantly; un-suspend after payment).
  4. Renewal invoices are auto-created 10 days before the paid period ends; the app shows a banner to the company admin.
