Privacy Policy — FlavorFlow ERP

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

FlavorFlow ERP ("FlavorFlow", "we") is a manufacturing ERP for factories, operated from Punjab, India. Website: flavorflow.co.in. Contact: +91 95016 06877.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email, company name, industry and phone number — used to create and support your account.
  • Business data you enter: products, batches, inventory, dispatches, reports. This data belongs to your company and is stored in a private database used only by your company.
  • Location: the app can attach a GPS stamp to dispatch entries when you allow location access. Location is never tracked in the background.
  • Camera: used only for scanning batch/QR codes and taking photos you choose to attach.

How we use data

Only to provide the service: running your company's ERP, support, and service notifications. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Security

Each company's data lives in its own separate database. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed (bcrypt). Optional biometric login and two-factor authentication are available in the app.

Data deletion

Message us on WhatsApp (+91 95016 06877) from your registered number to request export or permanent deletion of your company's data.

Changes
