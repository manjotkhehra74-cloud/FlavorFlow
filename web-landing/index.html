GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions,
before you start.

Is FlavorFlow right for my factory?

FlavorFlow ERP is built for manufacturing units that manage production batches, inventory, dispatches and GST billing. Try the sample company for your industry to see the workflow before signing up.

How does the free trial work?

Create your company to start the Pro trial with no credit card required. Android installation will be available through Google Play after publication; Windows is in development. Registering starts the trial even while store publication is pending. Your registration confirmation shows the trial end date.

Can my team use it in their own language?

Yes. The ERP supports English, Punjabi, Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu.

Where is my company’s data stored?

Your company’s ERP data is stored on our hosted server in a separate database for your company, not only on a team member’s phone. Role-based permissions control what each user can access. Contact support if you need data export or deletion assistance.

How do I pay after the trial?

Choose monthly or yearly billing and request a company invoice. Pay by NEFT, IMPS, RTGS (minimum ₹2 lakh), or cheque using verified beneficiary details. Share your company code, invoice number and payment reference with support; activation follows verification or cheque clearance. UPI and net banking are planned but not enabled yet. Optional UPI AutoPay / eNACH will require provider setup, bank eligibility and your explicit authorisation. Credit/debit cards are not offered, and registering does not authorise automatic debits.
